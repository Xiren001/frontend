Your Role in the Flow

The website team has two jobs in the proofread workflow: unlock the product for the proofreader (by adding links), and apply the website corrections after the proofreader signs off.

You add links
→
Proofreader reviews
→
Proofreader marks Ready
→
You apply fixes
→
You mark Done

You work exclusively in the Website tab of each product. The ADS tab is handled by the ads team.

2

Step 1 — Add Product Links

Before a proofreader can start reviewing, the product needs two links. Without them, the product shows a Needs links status and corrections are locked.

How to add links

  1. Go to Proofreading in the sidebar.
  2. Find products marked Needs links in the left panel — these are your first priority.
  3. Click the product. A setup form appears asking for two fields.
  4. Enter the PDP URL — the live product page URL on your store.
  5. Enter the Drive folder URL — the Google Drive folder containing ad assets and copy for this product.
  6. Save. The product status updates and the proofreader can now start their review.
⚠️
Do this immediately
Every hour a product sits in "Needs links" is an hour the proofreader is blocked. Check Proofreading daily for new "Needs links" products and add the links the same day they appear.
LinkWhat it isWhere to find it
PDP URLProduct Detail Page — the live product listing on the storeCopy the URL from the product page on your storefront
Drive folder URLThe Google Drive folder with all copy and creative for this productThe shared Drive folder created when the build started
3

Step 2 — Wait for Proofreader

Once links are added, the product moves out of "Needs links" and the proofreader takes over. You do not need to do anything during this phase.

You will know the proofreader is done when the product status changes to Ready for revision. That is your cue to start Step 3.

💡
SLA: proofreaders have 3 days
If a product has been past "Needs links" for more than 3 days and still isn't "Ready for revision," it is overdue. Check the Proofread Queue page — it shows overdue items flagged red. Escalate to your manager if a block is causing a bottleneck.
4

Step 3 — Apply Website Corrections

When a product is Ready for revision, the proofreader has logged all issues. Your job is to go to the product page (PDP URL) and apply each correction listed in the Website tab.

How to apply corrections

  1. Open the product in Proofreading.
  2. Click the Website tab. You will see a list of correction cards — each one is a specific issue the proofreader found.
  3. Open the live product page (PDP URL) in another tab.
  4. For each correction card: read the Location, Original text, and Corrected text. Go to that location on the product page and make the fix.
  5. Once the fix is applied on the live page, come back to Proofreading and click the green check button on that correction card. It turns gray with a ↩ icon — this means it is marked as done.
  6. Repeat for every correction card in the Website tab.
🚨
Only check off corrections you have actually applied
The green check means "this fix is live." Do not check off a correction as done if you haven't applied it yet. If you accidentally check one off, use the ↩ button to reopen it.

Reading a correction card

FieldWhat it tells you
LocationExactly where on the page to make the change (e.g., "Product title", "Bullet 2", "CTA button")
Original textThe incorrect text currently on the page — use this to find the right spot
Corrected textThe replacement text — copy-paste this exactly as written
SeverityCritical fixes first, then Medium, then Minor
NotesExtra context from the proofreader — read if you're unsure about the fix
💡
Work Critical issues first
Prioritize Critical severity cards first. These are the issues most likely to affect performance or cause problems if left live. Minor polish can wait if you are time-constrained.
5

Step 4 — Mark Done

Once every correction in the Website tab is checked off, you can close the product out.

  1. Confirm all correction cards in the Website tab have a gray check (applied).
  2. In the product header, click ✓ Done.
  3. The product moves to the "Done" group. Your work on this product is complete.
⚠️
The ADS tab is separate
You mark Done for the Website tab. The ads team independently marks Done for the ADS tab. You do not need to wait for the ads team, and they do not need to wait for you.
6

The Proofread Queue Page

The Proofread Queue page gives you a pipeline view of all products currently in the proofread stage. This is a monitoring tool — you do not take actions here, but it tells you what is happening across the queue.

What to look for

ColumnWhat it shows
Product nameThe build being proofread
LanguageWhich language market this is for
In proofread sinceDate the product entered the proofread stage
DaysHow many days it has been in proofread — turns red after 3 days
ProofreaderWho is assigned to review it
FlagRed badge = overdue (more than 3 days in proofread)
💡
Use this to spot bottlenecks
If you see many products flagged red, the queue is backed up. Escalate to your manager — this is not something you can fix yourself, but early flagging prevents pipeline delays from compounding.
