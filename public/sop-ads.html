Role Overview

The Ads team drives traffic, manages media spend, and is the primary driver of product testing velocity at Faszik. You operate across two product verticals — Jewelry and Funnel — and your decisions on spend allocation, campaign performance, and kill/expand recommendations directly impact the company's growth.

All builds you manage must be tracked in Myko Ops Hub. The trackers are the record of truth — if it's not in the system, it doesn't exist.

Key Responsibilities

  • Create and maintain build entries in the Jewelry and Funnel Trackers
  • Launch and manage ad campaigns for active test builds
  • Monitor daily performance — spend, ROAS, CPA, conversion rate
  • Work with the Web team on funnel builds; own creative and targeting for jewelry builds
  • Make or escalate kill/expand decisions based on performance thresholds
  • Nominate winning products for scaling
  • Log mistakes and contribute to weekly/monthly reporting

Build Creation

Every new product test starts with creating a build entry in the correct tracker. This entry is the permanent record for that product's lifecycle.

Jewelry Build Creation

  1. Navigate to Jewelry Tracker → click + New Build.
  2. Enter: product name, target market/language, assigned ads team member, and start date.
  3. Upload or link all creative assets (images, videos) to the build entry.
  4. Submit copy to the Copy Review module for proofreading.
  5. Set phase to Phase 1 until copy is approved and creative is finalized.

Funnel Build Creation

  1. Coordinate with the Web team — they create the Funnel Tracker entry.
  2. Submit your ad creatives and copy to the Web team for integration into the funnel.
  3. Confirm the tracking pixel and conversion events are correctly configured (shared responsibility with Web).
  4. Do not launch traffic until Web team confirms QA checklist is complete.
⚠️
No Informal Testing
Do not run ad spend on a product that doesn't have an active build entry in the tracker. All spend must be attributable to a tracked build. Shadow testing wastes budget and creates data gaps.
4

Campaign Launch

Before spending a single dollar, confirm all pre-launch gates are cleared. Launching on an unready product is a recordable mistake.

Pre-Launch Checklist

  • Build entry exists in the correct tracker and is in Testing phase
  • Copy has been approved by the proofreader for the target language
  • All creative assets are final and uploaded to the build entry
  • Pixel is firing correctly — verify in Events Manager before launch
  • Conversion events are mapped and tested
  • Landing page or funnel is live and passes QA (confirmed by Web team for funnels)
  • Budget has been approved if over the tool approval threshold (check Settings)
  • Campaign structure follows current account conventions (naming, ad sets, budgets)

Post-Launch Actions

  1. Update the build entry: set launch date, record initial budget, note targeting setup.
  2. Monitor for the first 24 hours for any technical issues (pixel failures, page errors, ad rejections).
  3. Confirm delivery is as expected — no abnormal CPMs or zero-delivery ad sets.

Performance Monitoring

Active test builds require daily monitoring. Performance data drives the kill/expand decision — the quality of your monitoring determines the quality of your decisions.

Daily Monitoring Routine

  1. Check all active build campaigns first thing each morning before making any budget changes.
  2. Review: spend vs. budget pacing, ROAS/CPA vs. target, CTR, and CPM trends.
  3. Log any significant performance changes or anomalies in the build entry notes.
  4. If a campaign is significantly underperforming within first 48h, flag for early review — do not silently burn budget.
  5. Update the build entry in the tracker with any changes made to campaigns that day.

🚨
Tracking Failures
If you discover a tracking failure after spend has occurred, pause the campaign immediately. Log the issue in the Mistake Log. Data collected during a tracking failure cannot be used for kill/expand decisions.
6

Decision Framework

Every build reaches a decision point: Kill or Expand. Use this framework to make and document these decisions correctly.

Kill Criteria

  • ROAS is below the minimum viable threshold after the full test period
  • CPA exceeds the maximum allowable cost after sufficient data
  • Multiple creative/copy iterations have been tested without improvement
  • Market or timing factors make the product unviable regardless of optimization

Expand Criteria

  • ROAS meets or exceeds target across multiple days/cohorts
  • CPA is within profitable range with room for scale
  • Performance is consistent — not a single spike that regresses
  • Audience saturation is not imminent at planned scale level

Decision Authority

Documenting a Decision

  1. Open the build entry and advance to Decision phase.
  2. Select Kill or Expand.
  3. Write a decision note: total spend, final ROAS/CPA, key performance observations, and the specific reason for the decision.
  4. If management approval is required, tag the relevant manager in the notes and set status to Pending Approval.
  5. Pause or scale campaigns in the ad platform to match the decision — do not leave live campaigns running for killed builds.
📋
Decision Rights Reference
The full authority matrix is always available under Decision Rights in the navigation. When in doubt, check it — making a decision above your authority level is a process violation.
7

Winning Products

Products that pass the expand threshold and show sustained performance are candidates for the Winning Products program. This is where Faszik's growth comes from — treat nominations seriously.

Nomination Process

  1. Identify a build that has passed expand criteria and maintained performance for a sustained period.
  2. Navigate to Winning Products in the sidebar.
  3. Click Nominate and link it to the original build entry.
  4. Include: total spend to date, ROAS over last 7/14/30 days, estimated scaling headroom, and your scale plan.
  5. Submit for management review — you will be notified of the decision.

Managing Active Winners

  • Winning products require more frequent monitoring — check performance twice daily
  • Budget increases above the tool approval threshold require prior management approval
  • Document creative refreshes and test iterations in the Winning Products entry
  • Flag performance degradation immediately — do not wait for a weekly report
  • A winning product that drops below minimum ROAS for 3 consecutive days must be escalated for review
🏆
Protect Winners
Winning products are the company's revenue engine. They get disproportionate attention, budget, and creative resources. When a winner shows signs of fatigue, address it proactively — don't let it decline to the point of crisis.

Reporting & KPIs

The Ads team is a major contributor to the Myko Ops Hub weekly and monthly reports. Accurate, up-to-date build entries are essential for report accuracy.

Weekly Responsibilities

  • Update all active builds with current performance data before the weekly report runs
  • Ensure all decisions made during the week are documented in the tracker
  • Review the generated weekly report — flag any data discrepancies to your team lead
  • Come to the weekly review with a prepared summary of each active build's status

Mistake Log
