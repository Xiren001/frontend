Your Role in the Flow

The ads team is the last step in the proofread workflow. You receive corrections from the proofreader, apply them to the ad copy in Google Drive, and check them off as done in the system.

Website adds links
→
Proofreader reviews
→
Proofreader marks Ready
→
You apply ADS fixes
→
You mark Done

You work exclusively in the ADS tab of each product. The Website tab is handled by the website team — you run in parallel with them, not sequentially.

💡
ADS corrections = ad copy fixes in Google Drive
The proofreader logs issues they found in your ad copy — headlines, body text, captions, CTAs. Your job is to open the Drive folder for that product and update those files with the corrected text.
2

When to Start

You can only apply corrections after the proofreader has finished their review and clicked Mark Ready. Before that, the correction checkboxes are locked — you cannot check anything off.

How to know a product is ready for you

  • Open Proofreading in the sidebar.
  • Look in the left panel for products with the Ready for revision status.
  • Those products have been reviewed by the proofreader and are waiting for your fixes.
  • Products with any other status are not ready — do not try to act on them yet.
⚠️
Check Proofreading daily
Products can move to "Ready for revision" at any time. Make it a daily habit to check the left panel for new green-status products. Leaving corrections unactioned delays the product going live.
3

Opening a Product

  1. Go to Proofreading in the left sidebar.
  2. In the left panel, find products marked Ready for revision.
  3. Click the product name to open it in the right panel.
  4. Click the ADS tab. This is where the proofreader has logged all ad copy issues for this product.
  5. Also note the Drive folder link in the product header — this is where you will apply the fixes.
4

Applying ADS Corrections

Each correction card in the ADS tab describes one specific issue the proofreader found in your ad copy. Work through them one by one.

For each correction card

  1. Read the Location — this tells you exactly where in the ad copy the issue is (e.g., "Headline 1", "foto n1 caption", "Primary text").
  2. Read the Original text — find this exact text in the Drive folder for this product.
  3. Read the Corrected text — this is the replacement. Update the file in Drive with this text.
  4. Save the file in Drive.
  5. Come back to Proofreading and click the green check button on that correction card. It turns gray — this means it is marked as applied.
  6. Move to the next correction card and repeat.

Reading a correction card

FieldWhat it tells you
LocationWhere in the ad creative the issue appears — use this to find the right file/section in Drive
Original textThe incorrect text currently in your Drive files — search for this to find the right spot
Corrected textThe replacement — copy-paste this exactly into your file
Issue typeWhat kind of problem it is (grammar, mistranslation, tone, etc.) — for your context only
SeverityHow serious — do Critical issues first
NotesExtra context from the proofreader — read if the fix isn't obvious
🚨
Only check off what you have actually fixed in Drive
The green check means "this fix is live in Drive." Do not check off a correction without actually updating the file. If you accidentally check one off, click the ↩ icon to reopen it.
💡
No ADS corrections? Still check the tab
Sometimes the proofreader finds no issues in the ad copy. The ADS tab will be empty. In that case, there is nothing to fix — go straight to Step 5 and mark it Done.
5

Marking Done

Once every correction in the ADS tab is checked off (or if there were none to begin with), mark the product as Done.

  1. Confirm all correction cards in the ADS tab show a gray check (applied in Drive).
  2. In the product header, click ✓ Done.
  3. The product moves to the "Done" group. Your work on this product is complete.
✅
You are independent from the website team
You do not need to wait for the website team to finish before marking Done. Your Done and their Done are separate. Mark Done as soon as your ADS tab is finished — don't hold up on their behalf.
6

Where Fixes Go — The Drive Folder

Every product in Proofreading has a Drive folder link in the product header. This is the Google Drive folder that contains the ad copy and creative assets for this specific product.

When you apply a correction, you are updating files inside this Drive folder — not anything on the live ad platform (yet). The corrected copy in Drive is then used when the ads are updated or re-uploaded.

Location in correction cardWhere to look in Drive
"Headline 1" / "Headline 2"The copy document containing ad headlines for this product
"Primary text"The main body copy file
"foto n1 caption" / "foto n2"The caption or overlay text associated with that creative image
"CTA" / "CTA button"The call-to-action text in the copy document
"Description"The ad description field in the copy file
⚠️
If you can't find the location in Drive
Use the proofreader's "Original text" to search for the exact string in your Drive files. If you still can't locate it, check the Notes field on the correction card. If still unclear, ask the proofreader — don't skip it or guess.

Common Mistakes

MistakeWhy it matters
Not checking Proofreading daily for "Ready for revision" productsCorrections sit unactioned — delayed fixes delay campaigns
Checking off a correction without updating the Drive fileThe fix is marked as done but never actually applied — the wrong copy stays live
Waiting for the website team before marking your DoneYou are independent — their Website tab and your ADS tab are separate; do not wait
Skipping a correction because you can't find the locationThat issue stays live in the ad copy — always resolve unclear locations before marking done
Marking Done with unchecked corrections still in the ADS tabUnchecked corrections are abandoned — they will never be applied
