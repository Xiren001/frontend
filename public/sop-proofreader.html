Your Role in the Flow

You are the quality gate between build creation and the teams applying fixes. Nothing moves to "Ready for revision" without your sign-off.

Web/Ads add links
→
You log corrections
→
You mark Ready
→
Teams apply fixes
→
Done

You only see products in your assigned language. You cannot access other languages' queues.

⚠️
You cannot start reviewing until links are set up
If a product shows a yellow "Needs links" status, the website team hasn't added the PDP URL and Drive folder yet. You must wait — the correction form is locked until links exist.
2

Opening Proofreading

  1. Log in to Myko Ops Hub.
  2. Click Proofreading in the left sidebar.
  3. The left panel shows all products in your language, grouped by status. Start with any product that is not "Needs links" and not already "Done."
  4. Click a product name to open it in the right panel.
💡
Where to find the copy to review
Each product shows a PDP URL (the live product page) and a Drive folder link (where ad copy and assets live). Open both. That is the copy you are reviewing.

4

Adding Corrections

Inside a product, you will see two tabs: Website and ADS. Copy issues on the product page go under Website. Copy issues in ad creative go under ADS. Add corrections to whichever tab is relevant — or both.

  1. Open the product. Click the Website or ADS tab depending on where the issue is.
  2. Click the + Add button to open a new correction card.
  3. Fill in all fields (see Section 5 for what each field means).
  4. Click Save on the correction card. It appears in the list immediately.
  5. Repeat for every issue you find — one correction card per distinct issue.
  6. When you have logged all issues, go to Section 6 to mark the product Ready.
💡
One issue = one correction card
Do not bundle multiple issues into one card. Each problem gets its own card with its own location, before/after text, and severity. This lets the website and ads teams check them off individually as they apply each fix.
5

Correction Fields

Every correction card has these fields. Fill them all — incomplete cards cause confusion for the teams applying the fixes.

FieldWhat to enterExample
Location Exactly where on the page or ad the error appears "Product title", "Bullet point 2", "foto n1 caption", "CTA button"
Original text The incorrect text exactly as it appears — copy-paste it "Compralo ahora mismo"
Corrected text The correct version — write the fix, not just a description of the problem "Cómpralo ahora"
Issue type Select the category that best describes the problem Grammar / spelling · Mistranslation · Tone wrong for market · Awkward / not local · Other
Severity How badly does this affect quality? Minor (cosmetic) · Medium (noticeable) · Critical (blocks launch)
Notes Optional — add context if the fix isn't self-explanatory "Informal 'tú' form is standard for this market, not 'usted'"
🚨
Always write the corrected text
Do not leave "Corrected text" blank or write "needs fixing." The website and ads teams are not language experts — they need the exact replacement text, not a description of the problem.

Severity Guide

SeverityUse when…
CriticalCopy is factually wrong, deeply offensive, or would prevent the product from running
MediumA native speaker would notice immediately — wrong tone, awkward phrasing, clear grammar error
MinorSmall polish — punctuation, stylistic preference, minor word choice
6

Marking Ready

"Mark Ready" is your sign-off. It tells the website and ads teams they can now start applying your corrections. Only you can do this — the teams cannot proceed without it.

  1. Double-check that you have logged all corrections for both the Website tab and the ADS tab.
  2. In the product header, click the ✓ Mark Ready button.
  3. The product status changes to "Ready for revision" and the correction checkboxes unlock for the website and ads teams.
  4. Your job for this product is now complete. Move to the next one.
⚠️
Don't mark Ready too early
Once you mark Ready, the correction cards lock for editing. If you mark Ready before finishing your review, you cannot add more corrections without asking an admin to reset the status. Review both tabs fully before clicking Mark Ready.
✅
No corrections found?
If the copy is perfect and you have no corrections to log, you can still click Mark Ready. This signals the product passed review with zero issues — which is just as valid as having corrections.

8

SLA & Common Mistakes

SLA

Every product must be reviewed — corrections logged and marked Ready — within 3 days of appearing in your queue. Products over 3 days are flagged red in the Proofread Queue view that management monitors.

DayExpected state
Day 1Product opened, links checked, review started
Day 2All corrections logged in both Website and ADS tabs
Day 3Marked Ready — no later than end of day
Day 4+Flagged overdue — visible to management

Common Mistakes

MistakeWhy it's a problem
Leaving "Corrected text" blankTeams cannot apply the fix without the replacement text
Bundling multiple issues into one cardTeams cannot check them off individually — creates confusion
Marking Ready before reviewing both tabsCorrections cannot be added after Ready is set without admin help
Waiting on a "Needs links" product without notifying anyoneDelays pile up silently — always notify website team after 24 hours
Writing vague location ("somewhere in the copy")Teams waste time hunting for the issue
